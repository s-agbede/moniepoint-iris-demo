# Successful transfer not received by the beneficiary

When a transfer is reported successful but the recipient has not received it,
share the 30-digit payment session ID from the transaction receipt with the
recipient. They can give that reference to their bank to help trace the payment.
This payment reference is different from a support-chat session ID.

If the recipient has already contacted their bank and the funds are still missing
after 24 hours, contact Moniepoint support for further assistance. This is an
escalation point, not a promise that every transfer will arrive within 24 hours.

The source also contains a temporary service-incident notice. That notice is not
included as an ongoing explanation: current transaction status and incident
information must be checked separately.
