# Adding and removing saved transfer beneficiaries

## Save a recipient

In the app, open Transfer, enter the recipient account number and select their
bank. Enable the option to save the beneficiary and confirm the recipient details.
Enter the transfer amount, continue, review the payment, and complete confirmation
inside the app with your PIN.

## Remove a saved recipient

Open Transfer, select Saved under the beneficiary selector, and choose Manage.
Find the recipient, select the delete icon, and confirm Delete Beneficiary.

Removing a saved recipient is a contact-management action; this procedure does
not describe cancelling or recalling a payment already sent.
