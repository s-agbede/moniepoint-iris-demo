# Money transferred to the wrong account

Contact Moniepoint support with the transaction receipt and details of the
account you intended to pay. Support can contact the receiving bank to request
a possible recall.

Recovery depends on whether funds remain in the recipient account, whether the
recipient consents, and the decisions of the recipient and their financial
institution. Reporting the mistake does not guarantee that the money will be
returned.

This guidance concerns a transfer to an unintended recipient. It does not
establish that a transaction failed or authorise the support assistant to move
money out of another person's account.
