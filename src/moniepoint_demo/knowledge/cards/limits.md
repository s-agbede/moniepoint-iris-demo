# Changing Moniepoint card spending limits

Open Cards in the Moniepoint app and select the relevant active card. Under
Card Settings, open Card Limits. Adjust the daily, weekly or monthly limit and
confirm the change to save it.

This article explains where to change card limits. It does not list the maximum
permitted amounts, promise that any requested increase will be accepted, or
establish the current limits on a particular customer's card.

Card settings should not be used to infer transfer limits or SAYT contribution
settings. An account-specific declined payment still requires its own checks;
this guidance alone does not establish why it was declined.
