# Reporting a Moniepoint card transaction dispute

A card dispute reports a problem such as an incorrect charge, goods or services
not received, or a payment the cardholder did not authorise. The cards team
investigates; a valid claim may result in a chargeback.

The published filing window is 120 days from the transaction date. Older
transactions cannot be entered into the dispute management system, although the
cards team may still query the bank on the customer's behalf. Resolution for
those older transactions is not guaranteed.

A dispute is a request for investigation, not proof that a refund has been
approved. The article does not establish a guaranteed refund completion time.
