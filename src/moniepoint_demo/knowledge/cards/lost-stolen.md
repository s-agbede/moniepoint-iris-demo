# Lost or stolen Moniepoint card

Block a missing or stolen card immediately using the Moniepoint app or by
contacting customer support. Report the loss or theft to support.

A replacement can be requested through the app or purchased through a Moniepoint
sales agent. The existing card must be removed from the account before another
card can be assigned.

These are protective and replacement steps. They do not confirm that a block
has succeeded for a particular account, or that a disputed debit will be refunded.
The published article does not specify a replacement delivery deadline or fee.
