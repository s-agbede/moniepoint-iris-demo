# Failed POS payment or withdrawal with customer debited

For a failed card payment or withdrawal where the customer was debited, the
POS operator should log the transaction for reversal if it is eligible.

The published guidance gives a reversal window of 24–48 working hours when
Moniepoint receives settlement for the transaction. That condition matters: it
is not an unconditional refund deadline for every failed payment.

If settlement is not received, the guidance directs the customer to their bank
to complete a dispense-error form.

This article does not list the eligibility rules or establish settlement status
for any particular transaction. Those facts need to be checked before promising
a reversal or choosing the next step.
