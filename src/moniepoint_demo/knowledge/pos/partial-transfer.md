# Card transfer withdrawal succeeds but onward transfer fails

A POS card transfer can have a successful withdrawal leg and a failed onward
transfer leg. Moniepoint's guidance describes the money remaining in the
business owner's wallet in this situation.

The operator can retry the onward transfer or refund the customer. If the value
cannot be found in the wallet, contact Moniepoint support for assistance.

The wallet balance and both transaction stages must be established for the
specific case before treating this as the applicable scenario. The guidance
alone does not prove where a customer's money currently is, and the demo
assistant has no POS refund or transfer execution tool.
