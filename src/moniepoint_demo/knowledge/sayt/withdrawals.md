# Withdrawing from a SAYT plan

Open the active SAYT plan and choose its withdrawal option. Specify the amount
and the receiving account, then review the request before confirming inside the
app using the transaction PIN.

The published workflow describes the receiving account being credited and the
plan balance being updated after the withdrawal.

This article explains the app procedure. It does not establish the success of an
individual withdrawal, a guaranteed completion deadline, or a complete policy on
fees, rewards, and eligibility. Consult the applicable plan terms for those
questions; this summary does not resolve differences between public sources.
