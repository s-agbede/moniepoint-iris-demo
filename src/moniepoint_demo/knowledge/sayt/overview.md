# Understanding Save As You Transact (SAYT)

SAYT automatically sets money aside when a transaction matches the types selected
in a customer's savings plan. Contributions can be a fixed amount or a percentage
of the qualifying transaction.

The published guide distinguishes two product versions. Version 1 is for business
owners and uses fixed contributions. Version 2 supports personal and business
customers and allows selected transaction types, including transfers, airtime,
and bill payments, with fixed or percentage-based saving.

The guide also describes scheduled payouts, temporary pauses, and manual access
to savings before the scheduled payout.

This explains the product generally. It does not establish which version,
transaction types, or contribution settings a particular customer currently has.
Interest rates are outside this summary; check applicable current plan terms.
